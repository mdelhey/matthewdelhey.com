I am an Assistant Professor (term-limited) in the Department of Philosophy at the University of Toronto, where I also received my Ph.D. in 2024.

My research focuses on 19th-century philosophy and social and political philosophy. I also have interests in early modern philosophy, 20th-century European philosophy, and the philosophy of technology and education.

Currently, I am completing a book manuscript on Hegel's theory of institutions.
